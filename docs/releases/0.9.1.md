# Touch Bar Studio 0.9.1 — Consistent themes and native-sized preview

Appearance colors now follow the selected background and accent throughout the Studio editor, palette, Properties, menus, settings, launcher, and Photos views. Text and accent labels use contrast-aware colors for light and dark backgrounds. The simulated Touch Bar retains its dark hardware styling.

The preview maps saved logical widget positions and content widths into the native-reported width of the custom Touch Bar area. Submenu and Resolve navigation reservations remain represented. A uniform fit keeps the entire preview inside a narrower editor window, while pointer movement converts back to saved logical coordinates. Automatic layouts continue to align items left, center, and right.

The native capacity response currently reports width only, so the preview uses a documented 30 pt control row. The separate Control Strip is shown at an estimated 176 pt. These editor previews do not establish physical Touch Bar rendering or measurements.
