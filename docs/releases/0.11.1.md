# Touch Bar Studio 0.11.1 — Resolve control sizing and release handling

Resolve color, numeric, and timeline controls now keep their declared native minimum widths under horizontal layout compression. Color and numeric labels are drawn within the native view bounds and truncate long text at the edge. Releasing a color dial drains its queued adjustment before ending the gesture; a cancelled gesture discards pending movement.

## Verification and known issues

- Frontend tests, production build, native build, and the native policy self-test pass. The native self-test emitted Launch Services notification warnings in this environment, then completed its checks.
- Touch Bar presentation after reinstall still needs physical confirmation.
